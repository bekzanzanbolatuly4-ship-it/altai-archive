# Data sources and provenance

## Institution and services

- The archive’s short public name remains **Государственный архив Восточно-Казахстанской области**. Current legal procurement wording and direct contact record are noted in [OFFICIAL_MATERIALS.md](OFFICIAL_MATERIALS.md).
- The legacy archive site `http://e-arhiv.vko.gov.kz/ru` timed out during review on 12 November 2026.
- Current supervising authority and its contacts: [gov.kz department page](https://www.gov.kz/org/vko-uca?lang=ru) and [eGov archive-authority directory](https://egov.kz/cms/ru/articles/department_of_archives).
- Archival certificate application: [eGov online service K002](https://egov.kz/services/K002/) (sign-in required) and [gov.kz service information](https://www.gov.kz/services/3014?lang=ru).

## Branches and map

The branch roster and Russian names follow the six-page East Kazakhstan regional akimat act No. 142 dated 2 June 2026, supplied directly by the archive. Its appendices list nine district branches and rename four existing branches. Addresses come from the customer-supplied spreadsheet saved on 10 November 2026; the spreadsheet does not state an effective date, so addresses remain source-listed and are not used to build routes. Staff phone numbers and personal email addresses are omitted. The act and spreadsheet contain no confirmed coordinates. The branch map is therefore unavailable; only the main archive's procurement-listed address has a 2GIS search link.

## History and calendar

The `/history` page includes ten verbatim Russian excerpts from the customer-supplied `История архива.docx`, covering 1940–2022. The source's preparation date is not stated. The commemorative calendar now covers 2022–2026 using the original Russian and Kazakh entries from five customer-supplied DOCX files. Each entry is labeled as archive-supplied; it is not presented as independently researched. Paired Russian/Kazakh entries with differing numeric details are both retained and flagged. No English translation is invented.

## Images and sample content

`public/images/*.svg` assets are designed illustrations. They are not photographs, scans, or archival originals. The customer folder contained no standalone photos or embedded DOCX images. The scanned 2026 administrative act and scanned 2023 price-list copies are legal/service documents, not images of archival holdings; they are not hosted on the site. Catalog records, exhibitions, news and atlas points remain labeled as illustrative where applicable. See [customer material inventory](CUSTOMER_MATERIALS.md).

See `data/archive.ts`, `data/branches.json`, `lib/provenance.ts`, and `docs/OFFICIAL_MATERIALS.md` for source labels and unresolved conflicts.
