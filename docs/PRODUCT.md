# Product brief

## Status and evidence

**Confirmed by the project brief:** The public site should retain the institution’s name, Государственный архив Восточно-Казахстанской области. The intended context is the East Kazakhstan Regional State Archive. The design direction is Editorial Heritage × Swiss Design × Altai regional identity, with the palette recorded in the README.

**Not confirmed:** Legal operator and audience, official requirements, archive holdings and rights, languages required beyond the proposed Kazakh and Russian interfaces, accessibility target, integrations, hosting, budget, schedule, and any approved AI use.

**Proposal:** Treat every feature in `FEATURE_MAP.md` as a candidate until archive stakeholders approve it.

## Product intent (proposal)

Make archival discovery and research more approachable while preserving the reliability, context, and provenance of archival description. Any future public interface should distinguish archival source material from editorial interpretation and generated assistance.

## Candidate audiences (proposal)

- Visitors exploring local and family history.
- Researchers and educators who need reliable citations and contextual metadata.
- Archive staff responsible for description, access decisions, digitization, and publication.
- Community contributors offering material or contextual knowledge, subject to a defined review and rights process.

These are hypotheses for discovery, not confirmed user groups.

## Product principles

1. **Source before synthesis:** show the record, description, and provenance behind any interpretation.
2. **Access respects rights:** access state, restrictions, and allowed reuse must come from approved policy and source metadata.
3. **Kazakh and Russian are first-class candidates:** translation, terminology, and script handling need local review.
4. **Research remains traceable:** citations and stable identifiers should survive interface changes.
5. **Progressive scope:** establish catalog quality and access rules before adding AI or contribution workflows.
6. **Revisable architecture:** keep domain and storage decisions reversible until the official specification and database are assessed.

## Success measures to define with stakeholders

Possible measures include successful search-to-record journeys, metadata completeness, accessible task completion, use of citations, staff time for publication, and user-reported usefulness. Baselines, targets, collection coverage, and measurement/privacy rules must be agreed before these become commitments.

## Out of scope for this planning phase

No public launch, archive data import, account system, AI provider connection, digitization workflow, or claim of official endorsement is established by these documents.
