# Theme and localization audit

Inspect before changing anything.

1. Read app/globals.css and identify shared theme tokens.
2. Check both light and dark theme definitions.
3. Find hardcoded colors and inconsistent surfaces.
4. Check RU, KZ and EN in locales/messages.ts.
5. Look for missing translations and hardcoded UI strings.
6. Check place-name consistency.
7. Inspect contrast, focus states and text overflow.
8. Check desktop, tablet and mobile layouts.
9. Do not invent archival facts or translations of original documents.
10. Report issues by severity before implementing fixes.

Preserve ALTAI ARCHIVE's Editorial Heritage × Swiss Design × Altai Identity.
Do not redesign the hero without a specific reason.
